How to play

Touch and drag to aim. The cannon points at your finger, and farther away means a stronger shot. Let go to fire.

The number on a block is its health. Each ball that hits it takes off 1.

Earn coins every level and spend them on Upgrades to fire more balls per shot.

Red blocks explode and chain into neighbors.

Every block in one shot raises your combo. Fewer shots earn more stars. You never run out.
